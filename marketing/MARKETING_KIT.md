## 6. Objection handling

### "Is this only for beginners?"

No. Beginners get a structured foundation; experienced readers get more precise control of pressure, timing, positioning, and consistency.

### "Is it just a list of tricks?"

No. The book is organized as a system: anatomy, build-up, two core techniques, refinements, combinations, control, positioning, and four complete sequences.

### "Will this work for everyone?"

No technique is universal. The playbook gives strong starting points and repeatedly emphasizes observation, communication, gradual adjustment, comfort, and individual preference.

### "Is it explicit?"

It contains direct adult educational language and simplified anatomy and technique illustrations. It does not use explicit photography.

### "What do I receive?"

A 24-page digital PDF delivered after checkout, ready to read on a phone, tablet, or computer.

### "Is this medical advice?"

No. It is adult educational content and is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
